Signal and execution assumptions

Only the last three completed candles are used. Length means high minus low, including wicks. Candle 2 is sharp when its range is strictly greater than % of the sum of all three ranges, and its body |close − open| is strictly greater than 80% of its own range. No earlier history or color filter is used; zero-range candles cannot be sharp.

Both sharp colors can generate buys, regardless of candle 3’s color. The buy limit is close 3, stop is low 1, and take profit is high 2. Only stop < entry < target qualifies. Orders activate after candle 3 and expire after {{EXPIRY}} minutes if unfilled, for every signal timeframe. Filled positions remain open until an exit or the end of available data.

Each order uses 1,000 units of the quote currency as independent notional and can overlap others. This is a setup study without an account balance or capital constraint. Net P&L deducts all incurred trading fees including open entry fees and includes available historical futures funding; unrealized gains/losses are excluded. Win rate and profit factor use closed trades after fees and modeled funding. Leverage, margin/liquidation, extra slippage, queue priority, exchange order-size rounding and liquidity constraints are not modeled. Historical funding is included for Binance futures; Hyperliquid funding remains excluded from its original replay.

Fee schedules are tier assumptions, not authenticated account rates or a reconstruction of historical promotions. Binance's standard spot and USD-M fee assumptions were checked November 23, 2026. Binance spot fees · Binance futures fees · Hyperliquid fees.
