Signal and execution assumptions

A sharp body covers at least 68% of its own range and exceeds % of the mean high–low range of the previous bars of either color. Candle 2 alone must be sharp. A doji has no color. Initial history bars are warmup and cannot generate signals.

Both sharp colors can generate buys. Entry is low 3, stop is low 1. The target is high 3 for matching colors and low 3 otherwise. An opposite-color or doji third candle has its target at entry and is skipped. Only stop < entry < target qualifies. Orders activate after candle 3 and expire after {{EXPIRY}} minutes if unfilled, for every signal timeframe. Filled positions remain open until an exit or the end of available data.

Each order uses 1,000 units of the quote currency as independent notional and can overlap others. This is a setup study without an account balance or capital constraint. Net P&L deducts all incurred fees including open entry fees; unrealized gains/losses are excluded. Win rate and profit factor use closed trades after fees. Funding, extra slippage, queue priority, exchange order-size rounding and liquidity constraints are excluded.

Fee schedules are tier assumptions, not authenticated account rates or a reconstruction of historical promotions. Binance uses the standard spot schedule checked November 23, 2026. Binance fees · Hyperliquid fees.
