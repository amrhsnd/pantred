Signal and execution assumptions

Each complete three-candle pattern falls inside the live recording. Earlier candles supply the 30-bar warm-up. A sharp body covers at least 68% of its own range and exceeds {{DEGREE}}% of the mean high–low range of the previous {{HISTORY}} bars of either color. Candle 2 alone must be sharp. A doji has no color.

Both sharp colors can generate buys. Entry is low 3, stop is low 1. The target is close 3 for matching colors and open 3 otherwise. Only stop < entry < target qualifies. Orders activate after candle 3 and expire after {{EXPIRY}} minutes if unfilled.

Each order uses $1,000 of independent notional and can overlap others. The first recorded trade at or below the limit fills at the limit. From that print onward, the first stop breach exits at the observed price, or a target touch exits at target. Same-millisecond prints use ascending trade ID. Fills assume sufficient liquidity. Recording gaps can affect event order and fills. P&L excludes fees and funding. Open positions have no realized P&L. Win rate uses closed positions.
